Music for your Discord, controlled from one message, or by voice.

Spotibot plays songs, YouTube and Spotify links and whole playlists in your voice channel. Everything lives in a single queue message with buttons, and with Gaming mode on you can just say "Spotibot, play ..." without leaving your game.

Getting started

Three ways to ask for a song. They all do the same thing.

  1. Join a voice channel.
  2. Type /play and start typing a song (suggestions appear as you type), or !play africa toto, or paste a YouTube or Spotify link (songs and playlists both work).
  3. The bot joins, and the queue message appears. Use its buttons for everything else.
Tip: !playnext <song> (or /play with next: true) puts a song right after the current one instead of at the end.

The queue message and its buttons

Spotibot keeps one message per server and edits it as songs load and play, so the chat doesn't fill up. !queue moves it back to the bottom if it scrolls away.

Finding songs, lyrics, filters and history

Pick the right version

/search <song> shows the top 5 matches (original, live, covers, remixes) and you pick one. /play suggests songs as you type.

Lyrics

The Lyrics button or /lyrics shows the current song's lyrics only to you.

Sound filters

Bass boost, Nightcore, Slowed and 8D audio, from More, /filter or "Spotibot, bass boost". "Spotibot, normal sound" turns it off. Volume is levelled automatically so quiet and loud songs match.

History and replay

/history lists what played and who added it; pick one to play it again. /replay or "Spotibot, play what we played last night" queues the whole previous session. On Sundays the bot posts the week's most played songs and top DJs.

DJ mode

Off by default. The server owner can turn it on (/dj): then only people with the DJ role (pick it with /dj role:) can stop, pause, change modes, filters and volume, or remove other people's songs. Everyone can still add songs, vote to skip, and skip or remove their own.

Vote skip

With 3 or more people listening, Skip becomes a vote and needs a majority. The person who added the song, and DJs, skip straight away.

Gaming mode

Control the music by voice while you play. No alt-tabbing, no typing.

Turn it on or off

Press Gaming mode on the queue message, type !gaming on / !gaming off, or use /gaming. The setting is remembered per server. The bot must be in your voice channel (/join or any /play).

How to talk to it

Start with "Spotibot", then the command: "Spotibot, play Africa by Toto". Saying "by <artist>" helps it pick the right song. You can also say "Spotibot", pause, then the command within 8 seconds. What it heard shows up on the queue message.

Privacy

While Gaming mode is on, what people say in that voice channel is turned into text by a speech server run by whoever hosts this bot (it never goes to an outside company). Audio is processed in memory and not saved, and only lines that start with "Spotibot" are acted on or logged. Let people in the channel know, and turn it off any time.

Radio, playlists and staying in the channel

Radio

With Radio on, when the last song ends the bot keeps going with similar songs (marked "(radio)" in the queue). !radio now or "Spotibot, play something like this" queues songs like the current one straight away. !radio off to stop.

Playlists

Paste a Spotify or YouTube playlist link into /play. If the server has Jellyfin set up, !playlists lists its playlists and /playlist plays one (with autocomplete).

Staying in the channel

The bot stays after the queue ends, so you can keep asking for songs. It leaves when someone presses Leave (or !leave, "Spotibot, leave"), after the idle timer runs out (30 minutes with nothing playing unless you change it with the Leave after idle dropdown; "never" is an option), or after 5 minutes alone. Paused counts as nothing playing.

Running your own Spotibot

The bot works on its own. The extras are optional services you can add later. Code and setup: github.com/Toe-Fur/Spotibot.

ModeYou runYou get
Classic (default)Just the bot (docker-compose.example.yml) Everything except Radio, Gaming mode and Jellyfin. Each song is downloaded with yt-dlp before it plays.
Streaming+ ytmusic-stream-server and bgutil-pot Songs start in about 5 s (instantly when cached), the next songs pre-load, Radio, suggestions in /play.
Gaming mode+ spotibot-sttVoice commands, speech-to-text on your own machine.
JellyfinYour Jellyfin server + API keyPlaylists and the Save button.

All of it in one file: docker-compose.streaming.example.yml. See companion/README.md for details. YouTube needs a signed-in session from most servers: put an exported cookies.txt (youtube.com and google.com cookies) in config/.
